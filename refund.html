Refund policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

If we cannot deliver

If we cannot deliver your order, we refund the full amount you paid, by the same method you used.

After delivery

Once your plan is delivered and working, we do not give cash refunds.

If it stops working

Your plan is covered for the whole period you paid for. If it stops working, message us and we will do one of these:

  • fix it,
  • replace it with a working one, or
  • add the days you lost to your plan.

If none of these is possible, we can move you to another plan of the same value.

How to ask

Message us on WhatsApp +961 76 970 994 or email help@teshrij.xyz, from the number you ordered with, and tell us what happened. We reply within 10 minutes between 10am and 11pm.
